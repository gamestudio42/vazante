# GameStudio42 Engineering Standards (Claude Code)

Read and follow `AGENTS.md` in this workspace.

If this workspace was initialized with `gs42-standards`, also read:

- `.gs42/standards/AGENTS.md`
- Selected profiles under `.gs42/standards/profiles/`
- Applicable files under `.gs42/standards/rules/`
- `.gs42/standards/skills/` when the task matches a procedure

Repository files in the current tree remain authoritative for
repository-specific conventions.

Do not push, merge, rebase, or force-push without explicit authorization.
Do not delete published games, wipe uploads, or reset production data
without approval.
